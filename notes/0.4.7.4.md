# Battlefield 1943 Reborn 0.4.7.4: the hotfix

Hotfix 0.4.7.4 is out for everyone. If you play Reborn, you already have it: the game updates itself at its next
start. Please read the known issues first.

Download and release notes: https://github.com/TekRantGaming/Battlefield-1943-Reborn/releases/tag/v0.4.7.4

Contents: [Known issues](#known-issues) · [Pause map](#pause-map) · [Server queues](#server-queues) ·
[Extras](#extras) · [Changes](#changes) · [Fixes](#fixes) · [Thanks](#thanks)

## Known issues

- **Invisible players or black textures.** We are aware that some players can become invisible, or some textures can
  turn black. This is being investigated. If it happens to you, go to **Settings, Graphics** and choose
  **Rebuild shaders**: your shaders are built again the next time the game starts, and this should fix it. If it does
  not, please be patient for the next hotfix.
- **Can't load into a server?** Third party overlays such as RivaTuner, Overwolf and others can stop the game from
  joining. Turn them off if you cannot load into a server.
- **Bots:** a reminder to check the FAQ in our Discord about bots being added to the game:
  https://discord.gg/8eakYSvBZS

## Pause map

- The pause menu now opens on a large map of the whole battle.
- **LB and RB** (Q and E) switch to the Field Report.
- **X** zooms in on the island.

## Server queues

- Join a full server and you get a place in its queue.
- A popup shows your position as it moves up, with a Close button to leave the queue.
- You join by yourself when your turn comes.
- The server browser shows each server's queue and offers **Join Queue** on a full one.

## Extras

- **Rebuild shaders,** in Settings, Graphics: deletes your saved shaders and builds them again at the next start.
- **Five vehicle achievements,** on either side: Steel Behemoth (a tank), Road Warrior (a jeep), Naval Warfare (a
  boat), Air Superiority (a fighter plane) and Death From Above (call in the bombers from an air raid bunker). Each is
  worth 10 G, and Death From Above 20 G.
- **A new achievement sound,** made on your PC from the Battlefield theme in your own game files.
- **A trademark sign in your name.** Your soldier name can now end with one ™ (Alt+0153, paste it, or the ™ key on the
  controller keyboard).
- **Watch video tutorial.** The first screen that asks for your game files has a button that plays our setup video
  right in the launcher.
- **Linux: host your own Custom Match,** online or on your LAN. By SmokeDoggy044.

## Changes

- **Suicide is now REDEPLOY** in the pause menu.
- **Server labels:** our servers read REBORN OFFICIAL SERVER, and community servers we vouch for read
  TRUSTED COMMUNITY SERVER.
- **VSync switches at once,** with no restart, and the frame limit follows your screen's refresh rate. By DiXN.
- **Server owners:** the server manager's Settings has an Automatic updates switch, on by default. If you turn it off,
  updated players may not be able to join your server until you update it.

## Fixes

- **Fresh shaders after every update.** Each update now deletes all your saved shaders and builds them again, even if
  your settings were reset, and a half deleted shader folder is built again in full.
- **Sound:** plane engines no longer drop out every few seconds, the Effects volume now covers gunfire too, and sounds
  are no longer cut short at their end. By SmokeDoggy044.
- **Smoother rendering:** more of the drawing work is spread across your CPU, with faster lookups. By DiXN.
- **Linux:** VSync no longer stalls a hidden window, and Wayland is used where it is available. By DiXN.
- **HD textures** are taken out of Settings for now.
- **General bug fixes.**
